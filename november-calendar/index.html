Post 5
CAROUSEL
01
Goldilocks and the Three Bowls of Healing
02
Too hot

She tried to fix everything at once. New routines, hard conversations, a complete life reset.

By the end, she was exhausted.

Healing isn’t a sprint.
03
Too cold

“I’ll deal with it later.”

Later turned into three years.

Avoiding it doesn’t make it smaller.
04
Just right

One small thing, done regularly.

No complete life overhaul. No pressure to figure it all out at once.

The right pace is the one you can sustain.
05
The takeaway

Nobody gets the temperature right the first time.

You try. You adjust. You find what works for you.

That’s part of healing, too.
CAPTION
