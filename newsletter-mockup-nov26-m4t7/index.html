The Best of Summer Is Still Ahead

Hi Megan,

The evenings are staying long and golden lately, the pace has shifted just enough to notice, and there's something about eating dinner outside that makes the whole day feel worth it.

July can also swap one kind of hard for another. The structure that quietly kept you afloat all spring is gone. The kids are home, the calendar looks nothing like it did in May, and that little voice asking am I doing enough, am I enough did not take the summer off.

This month we're sitting with confidence: trusting yourself again, making a decision without three days of second guessing, and no longer feeling like you're running just to keep up.

🤔 Did You Know?

Self-doubt gets louder when your routine disappears. Your brain relies on predictable patterns to feel safe, so when summer scrambles your schedule, the inner critic gets more airtime than usual.

  • Make a decision and let it stick. Pick the restaurant, send the text, choose the activity, then don't look back.
  • Notice what you actually handled today. Lunch got figured out, the hard conversation happened, things kept moving. That counts more than it feels like it does.
  • Talk to yourself like someone you're rooting for. You'd never tell a friend she's falling apart because she had a hard Tuesday.

🍓 Local Spotlight: A Farmers Market Is Exactly What You Need

A big jar of honey. A bunch of sunflowers. A peach you eat in the parking lot. No agenda, no notifications, just you and some produce for an hour.

  • Eastern Market (Detroit): loud, colorful, impossible to leave empty handed
  • Rochester Farmers Market: downtown Rochester at its best
  • Packard Proving Grounds Market (Shelby Township): cool space, great energy
  • New Baltimore Farmers Market: slower pace, in the best way
