The dip, explained

What softened in Q3 and what did not

Held

Platform leads and cost

Q3 delivered 76 platform leads against 63 in Q2 on a similar spend, so cost per lead fell from $98 to $89. September was the best month of the year at 28 leads.

Softened

Logged leads and August bookings

Google leads logged in the sheet averaged 31 a month in Q2 and 26 in July and August. The booking rate fell from 62% in July to 42% in August with the same volume, a handling problem rather than a traffic one.

Why

Seasonality plus system change

Both the 2023 and 2024 archives dip from June to September and rebound 35 to 45% in October. On top of that, the CRM switched in August and the new patient system went live 1 October, so logging and follow-up were disrupted.

SharesSent to a friend or a story. Primary signal for Humour posts.
Website visits and DMsThe soft CTA worked. A private message is the most natural version of reaching out.
