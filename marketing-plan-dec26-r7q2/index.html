How it comes together

Four steps carry a person from a search to a booked consultation.

↓ ↓ ↓ ↓
Consultation bookedThe main KPI
Step 1 · Awareness

People find Wellspring when they search for help.

What we do
Fix broken pages, build local pages, add the questions people ask, list accepted insurers, keep the Google Business Profile active.
How we measure
Organic clicks, keywords in the top 10.
Leads to step 2: a new visitor looks Wellspring up on Instagram before calling.
Step 2 · Consideration

People check who Wellspring is before they call.

What we do
Three pinned posts, about a third of posts on what Wellspring stands for, relatable posts, a link in bio to the consultation form. Every post ends by saying who Wellspring is and what to do next.
How we measure
Website visits from Instagram, saves and shares, followers.
Leads to step 3: they follow, save a post or join the newsletter, and keep hearing from Wellspring.
Step 3 · Engagement

People keep hearing from Wellspring until they are ready.

What we do
A newsletter that carries the blog, one theme a month across posts, blog and newsletter, stories every week.
How we measure
Visits per newsletter issue, visits per blog by source.
Leads to step 4: each blog and each issue sends the reader back to the website.
Step 4 · Conversion

A website visit becomes a booked consultation.

What we do
A clear way to book on every page, ads that land on a matching page, bids weighted to the days and hours that book.
How we measure
Booking rate, booked consultations a month.
The goal: more booked consultations, from more sources than Google Ads alone.
