Why the two years disagree
Learning 2023 is close to real demand. 2026 is substantially bought. 2023 was pre-engagement, so volume came from organic search, Psychology Today and referral. In 2026 paid budget is paced evenly by design, which mechanically flattens the curve.
Learning One partial pre-engagement year cannot carry a seasonal claim. 2023 covers April to December only. Any month-level seasonal statement is provisional until we have more years.
Next step Get the 2024 and 2025 years from Jessica. Three Januaries and three Julys separates seasonality from spend, and lets budget be paced against demand instead of spread evenly by default.
What does survive both years
Observation Summer is soft in both years. July and September were the two lowest months of 2023 at 74 each, and 2026 declines steadily from May to August.
Observation Autumn recovers, in the only year we can see it. October 2023 jumped to 121 and November posted the best rate in the whole 2023 file at 51%.
Observation December fails differently. 40 of 101 December 2023 enquiries went to no response, the highest of any month, while rejections fell. A follow-up timing problem, not a demand problem.
Next step Plan a December follow-up rota now. It is the cheapest fix in the dataset and the month is knowable in advance.

The substitution question, answered
Observation Psychotherapy is the stable base. From February onward it sits between 38 and 46 every month, a band of eight enquiries. It does not move seasonally in this window.
Observation Medication management is the volatile line. February spiked to 19 against a baseline of 9 to 12. That single month is most of the February peak, and it is not therapy demand.
Learning There is no substitution. When medication management fell 19 to 8 in March, psychotherapy rose by 5, not by 11. The lines move independently, so the services draw from different populations rather than trading against each other.

The same-day cliff
Scheduling rate by response lag
Observation Scheduling falls 25 points the moment contact slips past the same day, then plateaus. The damage is done in the first 24 hours, not gradually.
Learning The practice is mostly fast already. 63% of enquiries are handled same day and the median lag is zero. The 37% that are not lose about a third of their conversion.
Next step Concentrate the response effort in day one, not across the booking window. This is the strongest evidence behind the shortened window and the halfway reminder, and it reframes both. Harshit

The finding nobody was looking for
Observation Children and teenagers are 28.7% of all enquiries and convert better than any adult band, at 85% and 84%. Nearly three in ten enquiries are for a minor, almost entirely psychotherapy.

The real seasonal signal
Learning Seasonality lives in the presenting problem, not the volume. This is the answer the volume data could not give.
Observation Anxiety climbs almost monotonically from 35% in January to 65% in July, a 30-point rise. It is now the presenting issue in roughly two of every three enquiries.
Observation ADHD peaks in March at 37% and again in May, then falls to 20% by July. A March peak is consistent with school-year assessment timing, which makes it predictable and therefore plannable.
Observation Grief was the third most common issue in January and barely registers later, the sharpest single-month concentration in the data.

The five learnings that should change what we do
Learning 1 Completion is the whole funnel. An unfinished enquiry converts at 1% in 2026 and converted at 0% across 346 records in 2023. Friction removed from the form pays out at the full downstream rate, which makes form work the highest-leverage thing on the roadmap.
Learning 2 Speed beats persistence. Same-day contact converts at 71% against 46% the next day, and the drop happens inside 24 hours rather than gradually. Effort belongs in day one, not spread across the booking window.
Learning 3 The best-converting audience is one we barely address. Children and teens are 29% of enquiries at 85% and 84%, parent-initiated, arriving with the decision made.
Learning 4 Insurance has cost more than any other single factor for three years. The constraint is known and fixed, so the losses are now preventable rather than structural.
Learning 5 Seasonality is in the problem, not the volume. Anxiety climbs through summer, ADHD peaks in March, grief concentrates in January. Volume seasonality remains unproven and needs more years.
