Slide 1 (Cover)
Forgiveness doesn't mean
what you think it means.

Slide 2
It doesn't mean what they did was okay.
Forgiving someone and excusing them are two completely different things.
You can do one without ever doing the other.

You can forgive someone and never speak to them again.
Forgiveness and reconciliation aren't the same process.
That boundary isn't a contradiction. It's healthy.

Slide 4
Here's the real reason it matters.
Holding resentment keeps your nervous system in a threat state.
Forgiveness isn't for them. It's about stopping the cost to you.

Healing doesn't run on a calendar.
If you're carrying something, we can help you put it down.

30 JulThuStaticPillar 01A · ValidationIn progress

On-post copy

International Friendship Day

Some friendships are for a season.
That's not a failure.
That's how growth works.

Caption

International Friendship Day is for celebrating the people who stayed.
But it's also, quietly, for everyone sitting with the ones who didn't; or the friendships that faded not because something went wrong, but because you both grew in different directions.
That grief is real. The guilt around it is real.

And the circle getting smaller doesn't mean you're doing it wrong. Sometimes it means you got more honest about what you actually need.

The friends who let you be a mess and stay anyway; those are the ones worth celebrating today.

Notes

Friendship in your 20s and 30s changes radically and nobody gives you permission to let that be okay. That's the post.
